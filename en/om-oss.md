## What we do

The Italian Trade Agency (ITA) is Italy’s government agency for trade promotion and international business development. Through a global network of offices, we strengthen Italian companies’ presence on world markets.

In publishing, we work to help Italian literature reach more readers and publishers — through fairs, industry meetings, marketing and business matching.

For Nordic book-industry professionals, that means opportunities to:

- discover Italian titles
- find relevant publishers
- build new international business relationships
