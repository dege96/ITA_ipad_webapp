## Italian Trade Agency

The Italian Trade Agency (ITA) is Italy’s government agency for trade promotion and international business development. Through a global network of offices, ITA works to strengthen Italian companies’ presence on international markets and to foster international business relationships.

ITA provides information, market analysis, advisory services, business contacts, B2B meetings and support for international expansion. The agency also promotes Italian participation at international trade fairs and other industry events.

In the publishing sector, ITA works to increase the international reach of Italian literature and to create new connections between Italian publishers and international partners. Through fairs, industry activities, marketing and business matching, ITA helps Italian titles and authors reach new markets.

For Nordic publishers and book-industry professionals, ITA offers opportunities to discover Italian titles, find relevant publishers and build new international business relationships.
