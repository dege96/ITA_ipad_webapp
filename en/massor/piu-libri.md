## Più libri più liberi

Più libri più liberi is the fair for small and medium-sized publishers. The name means more books, more freedom. In the spectacular congress hall La Nuvola in Rome, hundreds of exhibitors gather for an intensive programme of book launches, author talks and debates. It is the perfect meeting place for visitors who want to discover unique publications and new literature beyond the major publishing houses.
