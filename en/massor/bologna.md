## Bologna Children’s Book Fair

The world’s leading international trade fair for children’s and young adult literature, illustration and multimedia content. The fair brings together publishers, illustrators, authors and agents from around the world for rights trading and creative exchange. Visitors get a unique insight into global visual storytelling through the renowned Illustrators Exhibition and awards such as the BolognaRagazzi Awards.
