## Salone Internazionale del Libro

Italy’s largest book fair and literature festival, founded in 1988 at the historic Lingotto Fiere. The event brings together leading publishers, international star authors and hundreds of thousands of readers in a wide-ranging cultural celebration with lectures, interviews and book signings. The festival also extends across the city through Salone Off, with events at theatres, libraries and public squares.
